# Reading list

Grouped by what you want to know. Each trail page also lists its own
sources.

## The whole subject

- **Printed Circuits Handbook**, Coombs and Holden, seventh edition, 2016.
  The materials part has chapters on base materials, on their components
  (resins, reinforcements and conductive materials), and a section named
  "Laminate and Prepreg Manufacturing Processes". Not opened for this
  site; listed from its table of contents.
- **IPC-4101**, the specification for base materials for rigid and
  multilayer boards. It sorts laminates into numbered sheets. Plain FR-4 of
  woven E-glass and epoxy is sheet 21. A paid standard, not opened.

## Recipes, from patent examples

Company patents give complete lab recipes: resin, bake, stack, press and
the measurements taken.

- [US patent 4,816,531](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4816531): a varnish without dimethylformamide, pressed at 100 psi
- [US patent 9,139,750](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/9139750): bake, stack of five, press schedule
- [US patent 12,077,667](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12077667): a press schedule with its ramp
- [US patent 5,726,257](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/5726257): bake and press for a 1 mm board
- [US patent 5,863,410](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/5863410): how copper foil is treated to bond
