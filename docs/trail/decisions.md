## Not decided yet

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Is the board a thick two-layer board, or thin layers in a stack? | On a thick board the weave barely shows. On a thin layer it is the main question. See [What it predicts](../model/predictions.md). |
| How long are the lanes? | One published budget is 1 ps of skew per inch for a 20 inch lane at 10 Gb/s. A lane of a few inches has far more room. |
| Must the board be flame retardant? | Without a flame retardant the board is G-10, not FR-4. See [The resin](resin.md). |

## What "from woven glass fibre" leaves two ways to do

- **Buy prepreg and press it.** Prepreg is cloth already coated with
  part-cured resin. This needs a hot press and a fridge.
- **Coat the cloth yourself.** This is the route chosen. It adds the resin
  chemistry, and with it the questions about solvents and waste.

The rest of the trail follows the second route.
